From a sentence to a logged meal

  1. You say it, your way

    Type a line, dictate with live transcription, attach a photo of a plate or a nutrition label, or start a hands-free voice call while you cook.

  2. The assistant uses the app's tools

    It looks up your saved products and dishes, searches the web for a product it does not know, and asks one short question when something is ambiguous. It never does arithmetic for you: totals, what is left and goals are computed by the app from your entries.

  3. You stay in charge of the journal

    Only what you say you ate gets logged, and you see exactly what was written. Suggestions wait for your tap on Log. Every entry undoes in one tap, and corrections keep the earlier version.

What it does

Live dictation
Speak into the message field and watch the text appear, or transcribe after you stop. Food words you use often are passed as hints.
Photos
Snap a nutrition label to read it, or a plate to get an estimate with its assumptions shown. Photos are sent for that request and not kept by the app.
Voice call
A short spoken conversation: describe your meal, answer one question, hang up. A second model reviews the call and logs the meals.
Web search
For a branded product it has never seen, the assistant searches the web and shows its sources.
Your products and dishes
Products you eat often are remembered, with shortcuts and dish variants, so the next log is one sentence.
Goals and weekly review
Dated calorie and macro goals, a remaining-today view, and a Monday review page with your week's numbers and weight trend.
Cost you can see
Choose the model in settings and the reasoning level per message; the app shows an estimate of this month's API spend.
Backup
Export a file anytime, or turn on automatic backup to your own Google Drive. Off by default.

Where your data goes

Trackly has no server and no account. Here is the full list.

On your phone

Your journal, goals, products, dishes, chat history and reviews, in the app's private storage. Your API key is encrypted with the Android Keystore.

Sent to OpenAI

Each AI request: your message or photo, plus the context the assistant needs for that turn (for example today's entries and goals). Sent with your key, under your OpenAI account's terms.

Your Google Drive, only if you turn it on

A copy of the database, sent over HTTPS to a folder the app creates, with the narrow drive.file permission that only sees files Trackly made.

No analytics, no ads, no trackers, in the app or on this site.
